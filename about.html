Direct Inspection Standards

Buying heavy earthmoving machinery requires transparent technical verification. Before recommending or advertising any machine, our team checks:

  • Undercarriage & Wear Components: Accurate measurement of track chain pitch, bush wear, sprockets, rollers, and tyre tread depths.
  • Powertrain & Hydraulics: Cold start inspection, blow-by checks, cycle time verification, and cylinder packings.
  • Structural & Safety: Boom, stick, chassis stress inspections, slew ring tolerances, and CE / UKCA compliance.
  • Provenance: Clean serial verification against plant security registers and documented service records.
